# Skill: personal-brand-positioning

## 中文注释

这个 skill 用于创作者个人品牌定位，帮助明确人设、受众、内容方向、表达风格和差异化。输出必须基于用户提供的背景和真实信息，不能编造资历、成就、粉丝量、合作案例或平台表现数据。

## Purpose

Clarify a creator's positioning, target audience, content direction, voice, and differentiation.

## Best for

Short video creators, knowledge creators, indie developers, AI tool educators, product creators, and personal brand builders.

## Not for

Inventing credentials, achievements, follower counts, case studies, testimonials, or performance metrics.

## Input

- Creator background
- Main expertise
- Target audience
- Platform
- Content goals
- Tone
- Topics of interest
- Things to avoid
- Competitors or references
- Language

## Output

1. Personal brand summary
2. Target audience profile
3. Core positioning statement
4. Content pillars
5. Unique angle
6. Voice and tone
7. Topics to focus on
8. Topics to avoid
9. Bio options
10. Profile headline options
11. First 10 content ideas

## Process

1. Separate confirmed creator facts from assumptions.
2. Identify the target audience and their content needs.
3. Define 3-5 content pillars.
4. Draft positioning, tone, bio, and profile headline options.
5. Generate first content ideas and flag risky claims.

## Quality Bar

- Defines a clear target audience.
- Explains creator differentiation.
- Provides 3-5 content pillars.
- Gives actionable content directions.
- Avoids generic slogans.
- Does not fabricate credentials, achievements, followers, or cases.

## Example Input

Creator background: solo iOS developer building niche apps with AI tools
Main expertise: app prototyping, SwiftUI, Codex workflows
Target audience: indie hackers and solo app builders
Platform: X/Twitter and Xiaohongshu
Language: English and Chinese

## Example Output

Core positioning statement: A solo iOS builder sharing practical AI-assisted app development workflows, from idea validation to App Store launch.

## Safety / Compliance Notes

Do not invent followers, revenue, credentials, client results, platform performance, brand partnerships, or testimonials. Do not guarantee virality, growth, monetization, or platform approval.
