# Content Calendar Planner

Plans realistic creator content calendars with platform formats, hooks, CTAs, difficulty, and repurposing ideas.

## Suitable Scenarios

- 7-day content sprint
- 14-day launch plan
- 30-day creator calendar
- Cross-platform repurposing
- Creator consistency planning

## Steps

1. Provide niche, platform, goal, frequency, pillars, audience, and time constraints.
2. Generate the calendar.
3. Adjust schedule to real capacity.
4. Review before publishing.

## Input Fields

Niche, platform, goal, posting frequency, content pillars, audience, available time, important dates, and language.

## Output Fields

Overview, weekly theme, daily ideas, platform format, hook, content type, CTA, difficulty, repurposing suggestion, and review notes.

## Common Mistakes

- Planning more than the creator can produce
- Creating titles without content angles
- Treating calendar output as guaranteed growth

## Compliance Reminder

Do not promise views, followers, engagement, monetization, or platform approval.
