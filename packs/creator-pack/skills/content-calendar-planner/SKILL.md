# Skill: content-calendar-planner

## 中文注释

这个 skill 用于内容日历规划，帮助创作者安排 7 天、14 天或 30 天的内容节奏。输出要能执行，包含主题、形式、Hook、CTA、难度和复用建议，但不能承诺涨粉、爆款、播放量或商业结果。

## Purpose

Create executable creator content calendars for 7-day, 14-day, or 30-day planning cycles.

## Best for

Creators planning consistent content across Xiaohongshu, X/Twitter, YouTube, short video, and newsletter workflows.

## Not for

Guaranteed growth plans, engagement manipulation, spam calendars, or low-quality mass posting.

## Input

- Creator niche
- Platform
- Content goal
- Posting frequency
- Content pillars
- Target audience
- Available time
- Important dates
- Language

## Output

1. Calendar overview
2. Weekly content theme
3. Daily content ideas
4. Platform-specific format
5. Hook idea
6. Content type
7. CTA
8. Production difficulty
9. Repurposing suggestion
10. Review notes

## Process

1. Match content pillars to the planning period.
2. Balance educational, personal, opinion, product, and community content.
3. Adapt formats to the target platform.
4. Add hooks, CTAs, difficulty levels, and repurposing ideas.
5. Add review notes without promising growth.

## Quality Bar

- Uses a realistic publishing rhythm.
- Provides content angles, not only titles.
- Differentiates platform formats.
- Is directly executable.
- Includes repurposing suggestions.
- Does not promise growth results.

## Example Input

Creator niche: AI tools educator
Platform: YouTube Shorts and Xiaohongshu
Content goal: teach practical Codex workflows
Posting frequency: 5 posts per week
Content pillars: tutorials, tool comparisons, workflow breakdowns

## Example Output

Weekly theme: practical AI coding workflows for solo builders.

## Safety / Compliance Notes

Do not promise virality, follower growth, engagement, monetization, or platform approval. Do not recommend fake engagement tactics.
