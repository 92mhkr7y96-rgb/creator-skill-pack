# Xiaohongshu Post Generator

Generates Xiaohongshu-style notes with titles, cover text, structured body, hashtags, CTA, and risk notes.

## Suitable Scenarios

- Xiaohongshu tutorials
- Experience posts
- Product education posts
- Personal brand notes
- AI tools content

## Steps

1. Provide topic, audience, content type, product or experience, tone, key points, personal angle, CTA, and language.
2. Generate post options.
3. Verify product experience and disclosures.
4. Publish only after platform review.

## Input Fields

Topic, audience, content type, product or experience, tone, key points, personal angle, CTA, and language.

## Output Fields

Titles, cover text, opening, main body, sections, bullet summary, hashtags, CTA, tone variants, and risky wording.

## Common Mistakes

- Writing exaggerated titles
- Faking personal experience
- Hiding sponsorship or affiliate relationships

## Compliance Reminder

Disclose ads, sponsorships, affiliate links, and material relationships when required.
