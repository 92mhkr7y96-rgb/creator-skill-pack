# Skill: xiaohongshu-post-generator

## 中文注释

这个 skill 用于小红书笔记生成，适合教程、经验、种草、测评和个人 IP 内容。输出不能伪造使用体验、测评结果、品牌合作、广告关系、赞助关系或平台表现；涉及产品推荐时必须提醒用户核实真实体验。

## Purpose

Generate Xiaohongshu notes with title options, cover text, opening paragraph, structured body, hashtags, CTA, and tone variants.

## Best for

Xiaohongshu creators, product educators, personal brand builders, AI tools educators, and ecommerce content creators.

## Not for

Fake reviews, fake product experiences, fake collaborations, undisclosed ads, plagiarism, or deceptive recommendations.

## Input

- Topic
- Target audience
- Content type
- Product or experience
- Tone
- Key points
- Personal angle
- CTA
- Language

## Output

1. Title options
2. Cover text options
3. Opening paragraph
4. Main post body
5. Structured sections
6. Bullet-point summary
7. Hashtag suggestions
8. CTA
9. Alternative tones
10. Risky wording to avoid

## Process

1. Identify the note type and audience need.
2. Draft claim-safe titles and cover text.
3. Build an easy-to-scan Xiaohongshu structure.
4. Add hashtags, CTA, and tone alternatives.
5. Flag fake experience, partnership, or product claim risks.

## Quality Bar

- Fits Xiaohongshu reading habits.
- Has clear structure and does not feel like hard-selling.
- Titles are not overly exaggerated.
- Does not fake product experience.
- Does not fake recommendation, review, or partnership.
- Reminds users to verify real experience for product recommendations.

## Example Input

Topic: how I use Codex to draft app store launch materials
Target audience: indie developers and AI tool learners
Content type: tutorial
Personal angle: practical workflow notes
CTA: save this workflow

## Example Output

Title option: I used AI to turn my app launch checklist into a repeatable workflow.

## Safety / Compliance Notes

Do not fabricate product use, reviews, sponsorships, partnerships, affiliate relationships, results, or platform performance. Do not guarantee virality, follower growth, monetization, or platform approval.
