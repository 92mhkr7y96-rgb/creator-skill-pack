# Creator Skill Pack

Creator Skill Pack is an open-source AI workflow pack for content creators who need reusable planning, writing, and content iteration workflows.

It is designed for YouTube, TikTok, Xiaohongshu, X/Twitter, Newsletter, personal brand, indie developer, ecommerce creator, AI tools educator, outdoor gear, and knowledge creator workflows.

## Best For

- Personal brand positioning
- Content topic planning
- Short video hook ideation
- Platform-specific content generation
- Content calendar planning
- Content review and iteration

## Not For

- Low-quality spam content generation
- Fake engagement metrics or fake audience feedback
- Fake brand partnerships, sponsorships, or platform performance data
- Plagiarism, content scraping, rewriting others' work without original value, or deceptive growth tactics

## Core Skills

| Skill | 中文说明 | Main Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [personal-brand-positioning](skills/personal-brand-positioning/) | 个人品牌定位 | Define creator positioning, audience, content pillars, tone, and differentiation. |
| [content-calendar-planner](skills/content-calendar-planner/) | 内容日历规划 | Plan 7-day, 14-day, or 30-day executable content calendars. |
| [short-video-hook-generator](skills/short-video-hook-generator/) | 短视频 Hook 生成 | Generate short video hooks, titles, openings, pattern interrupts, and CTAs. |
| [youtube-script-generator](skills/youtube-script-generator/) | YouTube 脚本生成 | Draft long-form YouTube scripts, chapters, B-roll ideas, and CTA structure. |
| [xiaohongshu-post-generator](skills/xiaohongshu-post-generator/) | 小红书笔记生成 | Create Xiaohongshu notes, titles, cover text, structure, hashtags, and CTAs. |
| [x-thread-generator](skills/x-thread-generator/) | X/Twitter 长帖生成 | Create X/Twitter threads with opinion structure, tweet drafts, strong lines, and CTAs. |
