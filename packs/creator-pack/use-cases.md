# Creator Skill Pack Use Cases

## 1. Personal IP Positioning

Clarify creator background, target audience, content pillars, tone, and differentiation.

## 2. Short Video Topic Planning

Generate executable short video topic ideas and content angles for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, or Xiaohongshu.

## 3. Xiaohongshu Post Generation

Create Xiaohongshu-style notes with title options, cover text, structured body, hashtags, and CTA.

## 4. YouTube Video Script Generation

Draft long-form video structure, opening hook, chapter outline, script, B-roll ideas, thumbnail text, and description.

## 5. X/Twitter Thread Generation

Turn an opinion or lesson into a structured thread with tweet-by-tweet flow and strong lines.

## 6. Newsletter Topic Expansion

Expand creator expertise into newsletter issue ideas and reusable content themes.

## 7. Product Recommendation Content

Draft claim-safe product recommendation or review content based only on real experience and provided facts.

## 8. AI Tool Tutorial Content

Plan content for AI tools, developer workflows, automation tutorials, and tool comparisons.

## 9. Outdoor Gear Content Planning

Plan outdoor gear content for camping, hiking, trail running, overlanding, and field-use stories.

## 10. Indie Developer Content Marketing

Turn app-building progress, lessons, and product updates into content for X/Twitter, YouTube, Xiaohongshu, and short video.
