# Safety Guidelines

This pack helps draft and organize creator content, but every output must be checked by a human before publishing.

## Rules

1. Do not generate fake data.
2. Do not fabricate audience or follower feedback.
3. Do not fabricate platform performance data.
4. Do not promise that content will go viral.
5. Do not encourage fake likes, fake comments, fake views, or engagement manipulation.
6. Do not encourage plagiarism, scraping, content theft, or low-value rewriting.
7. Do not generate maliciously misleading clickbait.
8. Do not generate fake reviews or fake recommendations.
9. Do not fabricate brand partnerships, ad deals, sponsorships, or affiliate relationships.
10. Do not generate content that infringes copyright, trademarks, or likeness rights.
11. Users must verify platform rules and content truthfulness.
12. Add risk notes for health, legal, financial, or other high-stakes content.

## Disclaimer

This skill pack helps draft, organize, and improve creator content. It does not guarantee virality, engagement, follower growth, monetization, platform approval, or business results. Users must verify facts, platform policies, copyright ownership, and disclosure requirements before publishing.
